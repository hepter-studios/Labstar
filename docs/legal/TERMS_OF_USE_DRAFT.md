# Terms of Use — Pre-Release Draft

**Status:** planning draft for Labstar's future public product. This document is not presented as final commercial legal terms and should receive professional legal review before broad commercial launch.

Updated: **August 13, 2026**.

## 1. Product model

Labstar is being developed as a public product that supports multiple organizations.

Organizations, channels, projects, connected repositories, files and internal activity are intended to remain private by default unless an authorized user deliberately enables a public surface.

## 2. Accounts and organizations

Users may authenticate through supported identity providers and may belong to one or more Labstar organizations.

Organization owners and administrators are responsible for managing membership, permissions and integrations for their organization.

Access to an organization does not grant permission to access another organization.

## 3. Acceptable use

Users should not use Labstar to:

- violate applicable law;
- attempt unauthorized access to another account, organization or system;
- distribute malware or abuse platform infrastructure;
- intentionally interfere with service availability;
- publish private organization data without authorization;
- misuse connected third-party services or credentials.

## 4. Integrations

Labstar may connect to third-party services such as GitHub and identity providers.

Integration access should use the minimum practical permissions for the feature being enabled. Third-party services remain subject to their own terms and policies.

Removing an integration may stop future synchronization without immediately deleting historical records that must remain for product, audit or retention reasons. Final retention behavior must be documented before commercial launch.

## 5. Public developer profiles

A future public developer profile is planned as an optional feature.

The intended rules are:

- disabled by default;
- explicit opt-in by the individual user;
- preview before publication;
- no automatic exposure of private repository or private organization activity;
- ability to unpublish.

## 6. Organization content

Users and organizations remain responsible for content they submit to Labstar and for having the rights necessary to submit it.

Labstar should process organization content only as required to provide, secure and operate the service and any features the organization enables.

Final commercial terms should define ownership, processing rights, export and deletion obligations precisely.

## 7. Source repository and product rights

The Labstar source repository is publicly visible for product transparency and development visibility.

Public visibility of source code does not by itself grant a software license. Unless and until a separate license is published, copying, redistribution, relicensing and commercial reuse are not automatically authorized by repository visibility.

The repository software-license strategy is separate from these service Terms of Use.

## 8. Pre-release software

During active development, features may change, be incomplete or be unavailable. Preview builds may not be appropriate for critical production workflows.

Broad commercial availability should only occur after the release, security, privacy, operational and legal milestones described in the public roadmaps are satisfied.

## 9. Security

Security reporting is described in [`../../SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md).

Users must not knowingly publish credentials, private keys, access tokens or sensitive organization data in public project channels.

## 10. Suspension and misuse

Future commercial terms should allow Hepter Studios to restrict access when reasonably necessary to protect users, organizations, the service or third-party systems from abuse or security risk.

The final policy should define notice, appeal and data-export expectations where appropriate.

## 11. Business plans and enterprise agreements

Future paid plans may have limits, support levels and organization features that differ by plan.

An executed enterprise agreement may supersede conflicting public service terms for that customer.

## 12. Changes to terms

Final commercial terms should define how material changes are communicated and when updated terms take effect.

## 13. Legal review required

Before this document is used as binding commercial terms, Hepter Studios should obtain professional legal review appropriate to launch jurisdictions, including Brazilian law/LGPD and other jurisdictions served by the product.
