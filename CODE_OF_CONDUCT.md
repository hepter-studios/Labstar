# Labstar Community Code of Conduct

Labstar aims to keep project discussions professional, useful and respectful.

## Expected

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks;
- give clear technical context and constructive feedback;
- respect privacy and confidential organization data;
- avoid spam and intentionally disruptive behavior;
- make room for contributors with different experience levels.

## Not accepted

- harassment or threats;
- discriminatory or demeaning behavior;
- publishing another person's private information;
- publishing credentials or private company data;
- repeated disruption after maintainers ask for it to stop;
- impersonation or deliberate misrepresentation.

This applies to official Labstar repository spaces managed by Hepter Studios.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

Sensitive security reports should follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead of being posted publicly.
